# Gap report: stability under resampling of job posts

Syllabus: generic test curriculum (64 subjects). Jobs: 509 open computing jobs; 200 bootstrap resamples (seed 11).

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Job coverage of the syllabus | 81.0% (95% interval 76.8-83.0%) |
| "Learn now" list overlap with the real list (Jaccard, mean) | 0.69 |
| Top 5 "learn now" overlap (Jaccard, mean) | 0.69 |
| Gap ranking agreement (Spearman rho, mean; 2.5th percentile) | 0.99; 0.97 |

## How often each "learn now" skill stays in the list

| Rank | Skill | Kept in resamples |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | .net | 100% |
| 2 | angular | 56% |
| 3 | kubernetes | 98% |
| 4 | gcp | 95% |

No skill has a significant rising / falling trend in the real data.
