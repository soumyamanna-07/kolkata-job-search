# Gap report: stability under resampling of job posts

Syllabus: generic test curriculum (64 subjects). Jobs: 506 open computing jobs; 200 bootstrap resamples (seed 11).

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Job coverage of the syllabus | 81.4% (95% interval 77.8-84.3%) |
| "Learn now" list overlap with the real list (Jaccard, mean) | 0.70 |
| Top 5 "learn now" overlap (Jaccard, mean) | 0.70 |
| Gap ranking agreement (Spearman rho, mean; 2.5th percentile) | 0.99; 0.98 |
